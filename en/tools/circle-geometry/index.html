Circle: Arc, Chord and Sector

Calculate the elements of a circle from its radius and central angle, such as the circumference, arc, chord, sector, segment and sagitta, or find the center and radius of a circle from three points on it, or from its general equation x²+y²+Dx+Ey+F=0.
