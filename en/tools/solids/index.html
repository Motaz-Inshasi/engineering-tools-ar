Volume and Surface Area of Solids

Calculate the volume, total surface area and lateral area where applicable of common solids, from the cube, cylinder, cone, sphere, pyramid, prism, truncated cone and torus, with the capacity in liters when using centimeters or meters.

📘 Formula used

V=πr2hV = \pi r^2 hV=πr2h
Volume of cone
V=13πr2hV = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 hV=31​πr2h
Volume of sphere
V=43πr3V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3V=34​πr3
Volume of truncated cone
V=πh3(R2+Rr+r2)V = \frac{\pi h}{3}\left(R^2+Rr+r^2\right)V=3πh​(R2+Rr+r2)
Volume and capacity
1 L=1000 cm3,1 m3=1000 L1\ L = 1000\ cm^3,\qquad 1\ m^3 = 1000\ L1 L=1000 cm3,1 m3=1000 L

✏️ Inputs

Use cm or m to display the capacity in liters automatically.
For a truncated cone it is the top radius, and for a torus it is the radius of the cross-section.
For a truncated cone it is the bottom radius, and for a torus it is the distance from the center to the center of the cross-section.

Example: a cylinder with a radius of 2 cm and a height of 5 cm.
